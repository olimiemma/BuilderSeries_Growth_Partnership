Why it happens

Nothing is broken. Three things are simply missing.

  • An RSVP is not a contact

    Names sit in Luma, beehiiv, WhatsApp and LinkedIn, and none of them talk to each other. Nobody can answer "who came twice, who's raising, who's hiring" — so nobody can sell to them.

  • Follow-up depends on you remembering

    Every partnership enquiry, sponsor question and DM routes to one inbox and one person. The ones you don't get to aren't lost loudly. They just go quiet.

  • Your members buy growth help — from someone else

    27% of small businesses have no real web presence, and 27% are actively shopping for a marketing provider. Your founders are in that market right now, and you have nothing to sell them.

All three are one-time setups. None of them become another thing you have to keep up with.
