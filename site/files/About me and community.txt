Apply for Membership

Trusted by 45,000+ builders across NYC

Real rooms. Not panels or pitch nights.
The Builder Series grew out of a simple frustration with empty networking — cold intros, loud rooms, and nights that looked busy without producing anything real. So we started hosting differently: curated dinners, runs, and roundtables where every seat has a reason to be there.

Today it's a 45,000+ member community anchored in NYC. Founders, operators, and investors who ship show up week after week — not for a stage, but for the introductions, follow-through, and luck that only happen in person.
